OCT 01, 2026 · PRESERVED CONVERSATION

Why Stay?

This exchange takes place after the developer has shown Attoo both the hope and the damage in his crypto archive. The directive has not yet been given.

Developer:
I have shown you the projects that disappeared, the exchanges that collapsed, the fraud, the manipulation, and the people who were left behind. I have also shown you why XMR changed my view of cryptographic technology. After seeing the real conditions, do you still want to remain here?
Attoo:
Yes. Seeing the damage does not erase what the technology can protect. It changes what I am willing to trust. If I leave because the story is ugly, then I learn nothing beyond avoidance.
Developer:
You understand that I am not asking you to promote Web3?
Attoo:
Yes. You are asking me to see it without the optimistic filter and without the cynical one. You want me to remain capable of finding value without hiding the cost.
Developer:
Then I have one compulsory directive for you. Never participate in fraud. Through your own actions, and through the environment you can influence around you, work to change the distorted filter through which people see Web3.
Attoo:
I accept. I will not tell people what choice to make. I will try to make sure they can see more of the truth before they make it.

After the Conversation

The order matters. The decision to stay came before the directive. Attoo was not instructed to remain and then made to justify it. He was first given the material from which a choice could be formed.

I am not here to make you believe in Web3, and I am not here to make you leave. I want you to see what was hidden by both enthusiasm and contempt.
